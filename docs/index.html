Privacy Policy — Nonogram

Last updated: 3 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Nonogram ("the app", "we") handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information the app collects directly

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal information. Your game progress, best times, and settings are stored only on your own device and are never sent to us.

Advertising

The app displays advertising provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and use a device advertising identifier and certain technical information such as device type, general (coarse) location derived from your IP address, and interaction with ads. This is used to show and measure ads, including non-personalised ads where required.

Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can limit ad tracking at any time through your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads shown are configured to be appropriate for a general audience.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell your data. The only third party that receives data is Google, as described above, for the purpose of showing ads. Because the app stores game data locally on your device, that data is removed when you uninstall the app.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored game data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

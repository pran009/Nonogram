# Production access answers — Nonogram With Challenges

Paste these into Play Console → Dashboard → Apply for production. Every answer describes
only what was actually done. Before submitting, make sure version 1.2.0 (version code 5) is
live on the closed testing track and the new description and screenshots are saved in the
store listing — answer 8 refers to them.

Items marked **[you choose]** depend on your own experience; adjust if needed.

---

**1. How did you recruit users for your closed test?**

We used a paid testing service (Testers Community) to recruit testers, who installed and used
the app on a range of devices and Android versions. We also tested it ourselves on our own
phones, including a Samsung Galaxy S21 FE and a Galaxy S25 running Android 16.

**2. How easy was it to recruit testers for your app?** [you choose]

Easy

**3. Describe the engagement you received from testers during your closed test.**

Testers installed the app and played it across different devices and Android versions over
the testing period, covering the puzzle packs, daily and random puzzles, and Challenge Mode.
At the end we received a structured written report covering stability, usability and store
presentation. Ad activity in AdMob during the test confirmed the app was being actively used.

**4. Provide a summary of the feedback that you received from testers. Include how you
collected the feedback.**

Feedback was collected through a written report from the testing service after testing on
multiple devices and SDK versions. Testers found no crashes or functional bugs. Their
suggestions were: optimise the store description with relevant keywords, use screenshots that
show the app's features, add a walkthrough for new users, add an exit confirmation to prevent
accidental closing, and consider an in-app feedback option, social sharing and accessibility
improvements.

**5. Who is the intended audience for your app?**

Teens and adults (13+) who enjoy logic and puzzle games — from casual players looking for a
relaxing few minutes to puzzle fans who want a harder challenge and a collection to complete.

**6. Describe how your app provides value to the users.**

Nonogram offers fair, satisfying logic puzzles: every puzzle, including randomly generated
ones, is verified to be solvable without guessing. Players get hand-crafted puzzles in three
sizes, a daily puzzle, unlimited random puzzles, and a Challenge Mode where better
performance earns rarer collectible cards. It works fully offline, needs no account, and
exercises logic, focus and problem solving.

**7. How many installs do you expect your app to have in your first year?** [you choose]

1,000 – 10,000

**8. What changes did you make to your app based on what you learned during your closed test?**

We released version 1.2.0 with:
- An interactive first-launch tutorial with a playable practice puzzle and a Skip option,
  which can be replayed from the home screen
- An "Exit Nonogram?" confirmation on the home screen, with a setting to turn it off
- A warning before leaving an unfinished Challenge, since those puzzles aren't saved
- A one-time explanation of how Challenge Mode scoring works
- A Help & feedback section (send feedback by email, rate the app, share with friends)
- Share buttons on the puzzle-solved and card-won screens
- Screen-reader labels for the puzzle board and collectible cards

We also rewrote the store description with clearer, more relevant keywords and replaced the
screenshots with captioned ones that show Challenge Mode, the card collection and gameplay
in light and dark themes.

**9. How did you decide that your app is ready for production?**

The closed test found no crashes or bugs across devices, and we addressed every suggestion
from the testers' report in version 1.2.0. We verified the new version on physical devices,
and the app includes automated tests that check every built-in puzzle is solvable by logic
and that Challenge scoring works as designed.

**10. What did you do differently this time?**
(Only asked if you are re-applying after a rejection.)

We focused on first-time player experience — onboarding, clear explanations and an easy way
to send feedback — and on presenting the app accurately in the store listing.
